About

The artist behind the look

For over 15 years, Sonali Sarode has been the calm in the chaos of Indian wedding mornings — painting brides across Nashik, Pune and Mumbai in looks that feel like skin, never a mask.

Her rule is simple: skin first, always. No pancake layers, no stranger in the mirror — just your face, luminous and breathing, on the day a hundred cameras find you.

From trial to touch-up, every look is built for the long day — tears at the vidaai, hugs from fifty aunties, dancing till the lights come on — and still flawless in every photograph.
