About

The artist behind the look

Sonali Sarode is a professional makeup artist based in Nashik, Maharashtra, taking bridal and occasion bookings across cities.

Her philosophy is simple: start with the skin, never mask it. Every look is built to feel like you on your best day — luminous, comfortable, and unmistakably yours.

From the first trial to the final touch-up, the focus stays on makeup that photographs beautifully and holds gracefully through hours of celebration.
